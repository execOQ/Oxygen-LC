## Change and visual verification

The previous hardware floated behind the tank. The revised fitting and saddle
seat against its surface, and the dial occupies the free area of the other tank
so the stock harness does not cross its face. A bevelled low-poly case and rough,
scratched metal use scene lighting. Subtle enamel/paint wear preserves readable
marks under the game's edge filter. The approved elongated edge marks and
left-to-right fill remain in use; the rejected inside-only layout is absent.

The gauge now follows upper-spine rotation as well as position. A skin bind pose
provides the reference orientation, avoiding calibration against a transient
spawn animation. Imported rig scale is excluded from metre-based mount offsets.
Standing, side, angled and crouched native captures were visually inspected.
The crouched gauge remains on the tank instead of disappearing inside the body.

Ten original 1920 × 1080 game captures replace the GitHub gallery, including
side contact, an angled close-up and the crouched recipient. The source run is
`.multiplayer-test/gauge-mount-animated-20261024`; its capture-evidence.json
records both peers near each screenshot. Shots use staged positions and a point
fill light; images and native prompt text are unretouched. English US input
produces the actual **E** prompt.

In the captured eight-second transfer, the donor changes from approximately
90% to 50% while the recipient changes from 20% to 60%. The donor HUD decreases;
the recipient's backpack arc increases. Capture snapshots are near the image
frame, rather than guaranteed to be from the identical frame.

## Coverage limits

This revision verifies the native orange-suit rig. Arbitrary replacement rigs,
all animation clips and all lighting conditions are not covered. The earlier
113-check network/cosmetic/soak run applies to the preceding DLL documented in
[RELIABILITY-2026-10-24.md](RELIABILITY-2026-10-24.md); that entire campaign was
not repeated for this visual revision. These results do not guarantee that every
environment is failure-free.
